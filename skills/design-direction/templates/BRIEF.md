## Hard rules

- One self-contained HTML file per option, with inline CSS and small inline JavaScript only. No outside scripts, fonts, images or trackers.
- System font stack only.
- Light and dark through prefers-color-scheme, with colour tokens on :root.
- Works at 390 pixels wide with no sideways scrolling.
- Motion only by moving, scaling or fading (CSS translate, scale and opacity, never layout properties), and fully still under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Real headings, alt text on every image, visible keyboard focus, and text at 4.5 to 1 contrast or better.
- Every fact is true and no feature that has not shipped is claimed.
- Use only the brand art in assets/ (if there is any). No stock photos and no generated people.
- Plain English, short sentences, no em dashes.
- The file starts with a one-paragraph HTML comment naming the design language, its reference points and its trade-off.

## Decision rule

- Directions (options) are drawn only when no look is locked: a new product, or the person asks for a relook.
  At most four, each whole and working, with a recommendation.
- Once a look is locked, every mockup is on brand and the variants differ in structure only (layout,
  navigation, wording, density), at most three per round. Each round ends with numbered questions for the person.
- The decision is always the person's. After the pick, the review loop (multi-evaluator) takes over.
